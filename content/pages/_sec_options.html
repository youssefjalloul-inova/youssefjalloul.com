What is an AI readiness assessment?

A structured evaluation of whether an organisation has the strategy, data, technology, people, governance and change capacity to adopt and scale AI. It ends with a scored map, a ranked use-case list and a roadmap, usually in two to four weeks.

Read the guide →

What is AI governance?

The policies, roles, risk controls and evidence that let an organisation use AI safely and prove it to boards, customers and regulators. It is usually built once, then mapped to ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF.

Read the guide →

What is a fractional Chief AI Officer?

A senior AI executive engaged part-time on retainer to own strategy, governance and vendor decisions without a full-time hire. It suits organisations that need AI leadership now but aren't ready for a permanent seat.
