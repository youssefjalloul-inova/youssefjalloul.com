Early signals

Eight quiet signs AI is already your problem.

You don't have to be “doing AI” for it to matter. These show up first in operations, procurement and the boardroom, long before anyone says the words “AI strategy”.

01

Staff are already pasting company data into free AI tools.Shadow AI usually arrives before policy does.

02

A customer or tender asks how you govern AI.It starts as one questionnaire line and becomes a deal gate.

03

Competitors are announcing AI-enabled services.Not all of it is real, but your board will ask about it.

04

Vendors keep adding “AI features” to your invoices.You're buying AI by default without choosing it.

05

Your best people spend hours on work an agent could draft.Reporting, triage, first-pass reviews, re-keying between systems.

06

Someone in IT has a pilot nobody in the business asked for.Technology push, no owner, no metric.

07

The board asked “What's our AI position?” and the answers differed.Four executives, four answers.

08

Your data sits in systems that can't talk to each other.AI amplifies whatever foundation it finds.
