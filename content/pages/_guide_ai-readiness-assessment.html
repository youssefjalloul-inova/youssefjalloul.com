AI readiness assessment for mid-market and enterprise organisations

An AI readiness assessment is a structured evaluation of whether your organisation has the strategy, data, technology, people, governance and change capacity to adopt and scale AI. For a mid-sized organisation it typically takes two to four weeks and produces a scored readiness map, a ranked list of use cases and a 12-month roadmap.

The six dimensions I score

Strategy and use-case clarity

Do leaders agree why AI matters and which outcomes it should move?

Data readiness

Is the data for priority use cases accessible, trustworthy and cleared for AI use?

Technology and architecture

Can your systems, security and integration support production AI?

Talent and capability

Who builds, who owns, who uses, and where are the skill gaps?

Governance and compliance

Are policy, risk tiers and human oversight in place and evidenced?

Change and adoption

Will workflows and behaviour actually change once the tool exists?

What you get

Deliverables

  • A scored readiness map across the six dimensions
  • Use cases ranked by value, feasibility and risk
  • Data and architecture gap list
  • Governance gap view against ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF where relevant
  • A 12-month roadmap with owners, funding gates and quick wins
  • A board-ready summary

What it replaces

Months of internal debate and a portfolio of unconnected pilots. You leave with a defensible shortlist, an honest view of your foundations, and a business case your CFO can fund. MIT's Project NANDA found 95% of enterprise generative-AI pilots delivered no measurable P&L impact. A readiness assessment is how you avoid joining them.

How it runs

  1. Week 1DiscoverExecutive and team interviews, document review, systems and data mapping.
  2. Week 2Score and shortlistWorkshops, dimension scoring, and a long-list of use cases narrowed to a shortlist.
  3. Week 3ValidateFeasibility, risk and data checks with the owners, and business cases for the top use cases.
  4. Week 4DecideExecutive readout, roadmap and next-step options. Smaller scopes can run in two weeks; multi-business-unit organisations take longer.

Questions leaders ask

How long does an AI readiness assessment take?
For a mid-sized organisation it typically takes two to four weeks. Smaller scopes can be done in two weeks, and organisations with several business units or regulated environments take longer.
What do you need from us?
An executive sponsor, six to ten stakeholder interviews, access to key documents, systems and data catalogues, and two working sessions with your leadership team.
Do you recommend specific vendors or platforms?
The assessment is vendor-neutral. It identifies what capability you need and whether to build, buy or partner. If implementation follows, any work through Inova AI Solutions is proposed separately and disclosed upfront.
What happens after the assessment?
You choose: run the roadmap with your own team, add a governance build, bring in delivery support, or keep me on as a fractional Chief AI Officer to own the roadmap with you.
