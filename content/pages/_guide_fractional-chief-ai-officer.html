Fractional Chief AI Officer for mid-market and enterprise teams

A fractional Chief AI Officer is a senior AI executive engaged part-time on a retainer to own AI strategy, governance and vendor decisions without the cost of a full-time hire. It suits organisations that need AI leadership now but are not ready for a permanent seat, and it can define that permanent role when the time comes.

Is it right for you?

Good fit

  • Scattered pilots and no single owner
  • A board that wants an AI position and a plan
  • A leadership team without the time or experience to lead AI
  • Mid-market to enterprise scale, with a mandate to deliver
  • You expect to hire a permanent Chief AI Officer later

Better alternatives

  • You already have a mature AI function with a leader
  • You only need technical delivery of one platform
  • You need a 500-person programme office

Questions leaders ask

How much does a fractional Chief AI Officer cost?
Market retainers generally run from about $3,000 to more than $30,000 a month (Christian & Timbers, 2026). My engagements are scoped to your situation, and the intro call gives you a realistic shape and range.
How much time does a fractional CAIO commit?
The commitment is set as a monthly rhythm agreed on the intro call, based on your size, risk profile and number of active initiatives, rather than billed task by task.
Can you help us hire a permanent Chief AI Officer?
Yes. A common pattern is to use a fractional leader to set governance and scope the permanent role, then start the permanent search while they are still in place.
Do you replace our CIO or CTO?
No. A fractional Chief AI Officer works alongside them, owning AI strategy, governance and vendor decisions while technology leaders keep running infrastructure and delivery.
