Why governance is now a commercial issue

It gates enterprise deals

Without documented AI governance, enterprise security reviews can stretch from 5–10 business days to 4–8 weeks (Security Boulevard, June 2026).

The penalties are board-level

EU AI Act fines can reach €35 million or 7% of global annual turnover for prohibited practices (Bright Defense, 2026).

It takes time to build

ISO/IEC 42001 certification commonly takes 6 to 18 months, so organisations that start early are better placed for customer reviews (Tarry Singh, Real AI).

Questions leaders ask

Is ISO/IEC 42001 certification mandatory?
No. It is a voluntary, certifiable standard. Enterprise buyers increasingly treat it as vendor evidence, but it does not replace legal compliance work such as the EU AI Act.
Do you provide legal advice?
No. This is advisory work. I work alongside your legal counsel and, where certification is the goal, your chosen certification body.
Does the EU AI Act apply if we're outside the EU?
It can, for example when you sell to EU users or your enterprise customers do. Start with risk-tier classification of your AI systems and confirm obligations with counsel.
Who should own AI governance inside the organisation?
A named executive owner with a cross-functional group spanning risk, legal, IT, data and the business. Governance owned only by IT or only by legal tends to stall.
