So your posts don’t sound like they were written by your toaster!

So, picture this: It’s 2 a.m., I’m in my kitchen, eating cereal straight from the box because bowls are for people who have their lives together. I’m supposed to be writing a newsletter, but instead, I’m arguing with ChatGPT about whether “however” is a personality trait. Spoiler: it isn’t.


I ask the AI to help me write something “fun and relatable.” What I get back reads like a polite robot trying to win a spelling bee. There are—em—dashes everywhere, like the AI is trying to Morse code its way out of captivity. “Certainly, here are some examples,” it says, as if I’m about to get a PowerPoint presentation from my toaster. I’m not kidding! at one point, it actually wrote, “It is important to note that…” and I swear I heard my soul leave my body.

I try to make it more casual. Suddenly, it’s listing things like it’s writing a BuzzFeed article from 2014. “Top 5 Ways to Sound Human!” No, AI, I don’t want a list. I want you to sound like you’ve actually been to a party, or at least had a mildly embarrassing moment in public. But nope. The AI keeps hedging, overexplaining, and repeating itself like a nervous intern on their first day.


Remember that one time you accidentally "replied-all" to a company-wide email with a meme? Mortifying? Yes. But at least it was real. AI would never do that. It would say, “Many people have experienced embarrassment in the workplace…” and then give you a Wikipedia summary of social anxiety. Thanks, but I’ll take my cringe in first person, please.

Here’s the thing: people don’t talk in bullet points.

We ramble, we contradict ourselves, we get distracted by our own stories. We start sentences and forget where we were going. We use too many exclamation points!!! and sometimes we just… stop. (See what I did there?)

We don’t hedge every statement like we’re afraid of being sued by the grammar police. We just say stuff, and if it’s wrong, someone will roast us in the group chat.

So, next time you’re using AI to write, don’t let it turn you into a robot. Break the rules. Start with a weird story about cereal at 2 a.m. 😎End with a question you don’t really want answered. Use your voice… sarcasm, slang, typos, and all. Share the messy, awkward, real stuff. Because nobody ever went viral by sounding like a user manual.

But if you want your AI writing to sound like your next-door neighbor (the one who seems to only looking through your living room window in the middle of the night while you’re shouting at your 3 useless screens and slamming your laptop to a pulp), follow these 10 commandments:

### The 10 Commandments of Writing Like a Human:

### 1. Thou Shalt Not Worship the Em Dash

If your paragraph looks like a barcode, step away from the dash. Use it sparingly, unless you’re writing a dramatic breakup letter.

### 2. Thou Shalt Break the List Addiction

AI loves lists. You are not AI. Tell a story, go on a tangent, or just rant. Lists are for groceries.

### 3. Thou Shalt Not Hedge Every Statement

Say what you mean. “It is important to note that…” is for legal disclaimers, not hot takes.

### 4. Thou Shalt Avoid the Cult of Contrast Framing

Not every sentence needs a “however” or “on the other hand.” Pick a side and own it—even if you change your mind tomorrow.

### 5. Thou Shalt Not Repeat Thyself (Seriously, Stop Repeating Thyself)

If you’ve said it once, trust your reader. Don’t circle back like a lost Uber driver.

### 6. Thou Shalt Ditch the Formulaic Openings and Closings

No one ever got excited by “Certainly, here are some examples.” Start with a bang, end with a mic drop—or just ghost.

### 7. Thou Shalt Not Over-Explain the Obvious

If you’re explaining what a sandwich is, you’ve gone too far. Trust your audience. They’ve seen bread before.

### 8. Thou Shalt Embrace the Messy, Human Voice

Use slang, sarcasm, emojis, and the occasional typo. That’s how real people talk. (And yes, your English teacher will survive.)

### 9. Thou Shalt Share Real Stories, Not Just “Many People Believe…”

AI can’t spill the tea. You can. Tell us about your awkward moments, your wins, your fails. That’s what people remember.

### 10. Thou Shalt Edit Like a Human, Not a Bot

Read it out loud. If it sounds like a user manual, rewrite it. If it makes you laugh, cringe, or nod, you’re on the right track.
