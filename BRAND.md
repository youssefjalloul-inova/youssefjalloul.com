Youssef Jalloul is an AI entrepreneur and venture builder based in Sydney, Australia. Across 20+ years he has built and exited multiple companies and created or implemented 200+ AI agents and proofs of concept.

**Long (about 120 words)**
Youssef Jalloul is an AI entrepreneur, venture builder and hands-on product designer and engineer. He is the Founder and CEO of Inova AI Solutions (2024 to now), whose platform BLDR OS lets enterprises and governments run AI inside their own infrastructure. He builds his own ventures too: Gus, an AI admin assistant that lives in WhatsApp, and Ikraa, award-winning software that teaches Arabic reading and writing in 24 lessons. He has advised governments in Saudi Arabia, Azerbaijan, Lebanon and the UAE, mentored 400+ entrepreneurs and speaks across the GCC and APAC. He writes The AI Glittch newsletter on LinkedIn. He holds an MBA from Monash University and a BBA from the American University of Beirut.
